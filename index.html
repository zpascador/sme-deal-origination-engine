SME Deal Origination Engine

How I built a weekly off-market deal-sourcing pipeline for SME acquisitions: from every company in a national database to signed letters, email sequences and tracked CRM deals.

Situation

An acquisition team with a company-database licence and a small staff, looking for founder-owned SMEs whose owners are nearing succession.

Problem

Tens of thousands of candidates, unreliable industry codes, personal letters nobody can write by hand at scale, and targets that may already have been sold.

Goal

Contact every eligible owner exactly once, with a specific and true message, and track every outcome in the CRM without copying data between tools.

A generalised case study. Every company, place, company number, message and alert shown is illustrative, and figures are approximate percentages.
